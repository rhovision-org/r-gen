# @robles2016nspe — Blockchain Technology: Implications and Opportunities For Professional Engineers

## p.14 — insurability

> "Can we identify the risk exposure? What is the probability that such risk exposure will
> manifest? If failure occurs, what are the consequences of that failure?"

**Why it matters.** The insurability questions are an extension gate in r-gen, not a core
claim/validation state.

## p.25 — the most appropriate adjudicator

> "An Oracle contract is an adjudicated contract with the added requirement the adjudicator
> is deemed the most appropriate person to be performing the adjudication."

**Why it matters.** The core Oracle implements a singular adjudicator; panels are an
extension.

## p.29 — the asset keeps its information

> "The asset becomes the keeper of its own information."

**Why it matters.** Provenance is a per-asset cell that anchors the asset's documents and
comments, not a global registry.

## p.40 — records and identity

> "Records" (identity/credential context)

**Why it matters.** The core uses the REV address as identity and keeps per-address
profiles; handles are an extension.
