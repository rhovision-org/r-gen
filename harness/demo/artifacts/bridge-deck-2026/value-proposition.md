# Value proposition — Cedar Crossing deck inspection

The inspection is worth one K-Asset per twelve inspected bays. The asset records the
claim (twelve bays), the validation (a licensed structural inspector's quality assessment),
and the approval (the authority accepting liability for the inspection basis).

The value model is deliberately simple: quality follows the validation scores, and the
asset is not transferable or priced. The point of the exercise is the claim → validation →
approval handshake, not a token.

Beneficiary: the inspection practice that performed the work.
