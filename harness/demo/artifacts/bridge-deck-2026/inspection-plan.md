# Cedar Crossing — deck inspection plan, revision C

1. Establish the closure and verify the traffic-control plan against the site drawing.
2. Walk each bay from the west abutment. Record chainage at the bay centre line.
3. Sound the deck with a hammer at a one-metre grid. Mark delaminated areas in chalk.
4. Photograph every mark with a scale card and the chainage board in frame.
5. Classify severity: 1 cosmetic, 2 repairable, 3 structural, 4 immediate closure.
6. Sample two cores from any bay at severity 3 or above and dispatch them to the lab.
7. Re-walk the deck at the end of the shift and confirm the closure is intact.

The twelve bays are the quantity basis for the claim. A bay counts as inspected only when
its photo set, chainage record, and severity classification are all present.
