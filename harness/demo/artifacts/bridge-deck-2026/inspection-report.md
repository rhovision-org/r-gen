# Cedar Crossing deck inspection report

Bays 1–4: no defects above severity 1. Hairline cracking at the east end of bay 3.
Bays 5–8: spalling at the north kerb of bay 6, approximately 0.4 m². Delamination mapped
across 1.2 m² of bay 7. Both marked and photographed.
Bays 9–12: efflorescence at the scuppers of bays 10 and 11; drainage is restricted.
Bay 12 shows section loss at the expansion joint, severity 3. Two cores dispatched.

All twelve bays have a complete photo set and chainage record. The report is issued with
the photo log and the lab dispatch note.
