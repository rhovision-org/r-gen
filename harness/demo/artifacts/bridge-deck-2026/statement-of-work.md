# Bridge deck inspection — statement of work

Project: Cedar Crossing deck rehabilitation, phase 2
Client: Cedar Crossing Municipal Authority
Inspection window: 12 working days

Scope: inspect twelve deck bays, the expansion joints at each abutment, and the drainage
scuppers. Each bay is examined for delamination, spalling, efflorescence, and section loss.
The inspector records a photograph of every defect, a chainage reference, and a severity
class. The inspection plan is revision C, issued with this statement.

Deliverable: one inspection report covering all twelve bays, a photo log keyed by chainage,
and a material-test addendum for any bay classified as severity 3 or above.
