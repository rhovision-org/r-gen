# Dispute grounds

The approval accepted the inspection basis but the claimant says bay 12 was reclassified
after the cores were read. The validator says the reclassification was not in the report
revision that was validated. The question for the adjudicator is whether the approved
K-Asset covers the revision that was actually validated, or the later lab addendum.

The dispute is about the record, not the physical condition of the deck.
